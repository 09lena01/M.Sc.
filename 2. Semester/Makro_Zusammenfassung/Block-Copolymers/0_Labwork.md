## Disulfide Bond
- redox-responsive - degradation
	- reduced to two thiol groups
		- reducing agents:
			- monothiol reducing agents (glutathione, β-mercaptoethanol), base as catalyst
			- dithiols - two sulfide groups, form stable cyclic compound when oxidized; basic conditions, short half-life in solution (dithioerythritol (DTE), dithiothreitol (DTT))
			- phosphines - nucleophilic attack of disulfide (tris-(2-carboxyethyl)phosphine (TCEP))

- dynamic covalent bond
## Poly(disulfide)s
- dynamic properties enables polymer to display high adaptability, stress resistance, self-repair & degradability
- synthesis
	- step-growth polycondensation-type reaction
		- oxidation of thiols
		- disulfide-exchange polymerization
		- regular polycondensation
	- ring-opening polymerization
- co-monomer to achieve degradability of other block
	- crosslink
## Cyclic Disulfides
