### Liquid Adsorption Chromatography at Critical Conditions-SEC (LACCC-SEC)

- combines the selectivity of adsorption chromatography with the size‑based separation of size‑exclusion chromatography
- decouple molecular‑weight (size) information from chemical‑structure (composition, architecture, interactions) information.
- Critical conditions are reached when _ΔG<sub>ads</sub>_ = 0 ⇒ _K_ = 1
	- tune one or more mobile‑phase variables (solvent polarity, eluent strength, pH, temperature) until the slope of _ln K_ vs. the variable crosses zero for the polymer of interest
