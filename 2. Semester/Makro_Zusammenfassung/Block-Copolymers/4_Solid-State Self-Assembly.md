# Theoretical Aspects
- system seeks to minimize free energy
- increase of entropy by mixing components is reduced in polymers - less mobility: less possible configurations
- increasing repeating units in polymer decreases entropic contribution to free energy
	- enthalpy dominates
- phase separation is limited to nanoscale

- χ: Flory-Huggins parameter (fixed for Polymer-Pair), N: degree of polymerization, f: composition (block ratio)
