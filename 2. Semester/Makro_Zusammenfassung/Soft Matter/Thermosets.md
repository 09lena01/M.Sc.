# Characterization
## Chemistry
- FTIR/NIR - conversion
- DSC - curing process
- solid-state NMR - crosslink motifs
- extraction process - sol & gel fractions
- swelling experiment - crosslinking density
## Material properties
- DSC - Tg of cured material
- TGA - stability and char yield
- DMA - G', G'', $\tan\delta$ 
- tensile testing - modulus, strength and elongation
- tear - crack resistance
- durometry - shore hardness
- microscopy - structure & morphology
