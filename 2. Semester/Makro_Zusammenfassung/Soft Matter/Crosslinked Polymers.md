# Definitions
## Crosslink
- short bond/sequence of bonds that link one polymer to another
- ionic or covalent bonds
## Crosslinked Polymers
- individual polymer chains are connected by bonds, forming 3D-network
- crosslinking influences mechanical strength, elasticity and thermal stability (usually higher)
- high molecular weights

# Sol-Gel Transition
![[Storage_Loss_Moduli.png]]
- Storage/elastic modulus G' - energy that can be stored in system
- Loss/viscous modulus G'' - energy that gets lost to heat production

- Sol-gel transition reflects shift from viscous-dominated behavious (liquid-like) to elastic-dominated behaviour (solid-like)
- loss tangent $\tan\delta = \frac{G''}{G'}$ 
	- $\tan\delta < 0.1$: highly elastic (thermosets, gels)
	- medium - high: soft solids, pastes, creams
	- $\tan\delta > 100$: liquid

	- measurement of  $\tan\delta$ as a function of concentration via mechanical testing at different frequencies
		- cross-section: gel point - frequency independant
		- above: viscous behaviour
		- underneath: elastic behaviour

# Solvent Quality
Flory-Huggins lattice theory
	- defines solvent quality 
		- interaction parameter χ
			- describes energetic balance between polymer solvent contacts & polymer-polymer/solvent-solvent contacts
			- swelling polymer to maximize solvent exposure vs. collaps to minimize it
		- Good solvent (χ < 0.5): polymer-solvent interactions highly favourable, chains swell
		- Theta (θ) solvent (χ = 0.5): favourable polymer-solvent interactions balance energetic penalties of polymer-polymer contacts, ideal chain
		- Poor solvent (χ > 0.5): polymer-solvent interactions unfavourable, chains collapse, potentially precipitate out of solution
# Swelling mechanisms
Swelling occurs when solvent penetrates structure, causing chains to solvate & expand the material
Dynamic balance between:
- osmotic pressure - drives solvent into network
- elastic retraction - resists chain stretching
Network properties that govern swelling process:
- crosslink density (increase: swelling decreases)
- solvent quality (increase: swelling increases)
- entanglements (correlation to Mn - Mn increases: entanglements increase, swelling decreases)
# Flory-Rehner Theory
"Given that polymer is permanently crosslinked & cannot dissolve, how much solvent will it absorb before mixing forces are balanced by network elasticity"
$\pi_{mix}+\pi_{el}=0=[\ln (1-\phi)+\phi+\chi\phi^2]+[\nu(\phi^{1/3}-\frac{\phi}{2})]$ 
- linking equilibrium per volume fraction ɸ to cross-link density ν  
	Swelling occurs via
	- mixing 
	- retraction
	- equilibrium is reached when osmotic pressure is zero
